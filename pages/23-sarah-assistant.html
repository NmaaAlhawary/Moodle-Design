What's the difference between voltage regulation and efficiency for a transformer? Dr. Omar said people mix them up.

They answer two different questions about the same transformer:

  • Voltage regulation is how much the secondary voltage drops from no-load to full-load, as a percentage of the full-load voltage. It is caused by the series impedance — the winding resistance and leakage reactance.1
  • Efficiency is output power over input power. The losses are copper loss (varies with load²) and core loss (roughly constant).2

Dr. Mansour's point in the lecture: a transformer can have excellent efficiency and poor regulation at the same time — e.g. at a lagging power factor, where the reactive drop dominates.3 Maximum efficiency happens where copper loss equals core loss.
