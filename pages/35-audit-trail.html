How a write earns a row here

1PreviewThe change is described. Nothing is written.
2Capability checkMoodle decides whether this person may do it.
3Impact countA destructive write states what it will destroy, counted live.
4ConfirmPressed once. An idempotency key stops a double.
5RecordThis log's row — who, when, what, and whether it worked.
6Live re-readThe change is read back from Moodle, so the screen shows what is really there.
You see changes in courses you can access, and no others — Moodle decides. Times are shown in your zone; the exact UTC instant is in each row's evidence.
