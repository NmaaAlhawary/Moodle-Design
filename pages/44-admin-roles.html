How verdicts are shown

Four answers, four looks — they never converge
Granted

Moodle said yes. The action is offered; the write still re-checks.

Refused

Moodle said no. Shown greyed with Moodle's reason — not hidden.

Not known

The read failed or is in flight. Still a working link — never a refusal.

Not enabled here

The deployment has this write switched off. A config fact, not a verdict.
